# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and evidence-led collaboration environment.

Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing experience and perspectives, avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct, and protect confidential or sensitive information. Project maintainers may edit or reject contributions and restrict participation when conduct threatens people, research integrity, privacy, or project safety.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Repository-specific contact details must be added before public release.
