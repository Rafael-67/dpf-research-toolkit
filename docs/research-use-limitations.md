# Research-use limitations

The framework and software are unvalidated research artifacts. DPF-RT must never be interpreted as calculating Delivered Protection, biological risk, residual risk, exposure probability, containment level, or BSL.

The application does not recommend controls or laboratory decisions and does not infer scientific conclusions. κ values and interpretive bands are descriptive. Narrative content remains qualitative.

The five included scenarios are fictional demonstrations, not protocols or safety guidance. They are loaded automatically for testing and may be supplemented by importing a valid `study-config.json`. The tool is designed for Phase 0 content/usability work with a small crossed evaluator panel; generalization to larger panels, recurring rounds, other laboratory domains, or real operational studies requires additional design and governance review.

Weighted κ, ICC, I-CVI/S-CVI calculations, aggregate RTLX/SUS dashboards, and the full Analyst panel are deferred to v0.2. Their absence must not be filled by interpreting current outputs as substitutes.
