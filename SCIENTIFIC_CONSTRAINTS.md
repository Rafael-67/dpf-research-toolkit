# Scientific Constraints

DPF-RP supports research on Delivered Protection. It does not calculate
biological risk or exposure probability, assign containment/BSL, classify
incident severity, provide correct answers, make regulatory recommendations,
modify taxonomies or responses automatically, reconcile experts
automatically, or emit scientific conclusions.

Dashboard outputs are descriptive. Missing data remain visible and scenario
classes are separate by default. Existing κ remains in the pre-specified
round-analysis view and is not generalized by the dashboard.

Historical sessions, responses, taxonomy ratings and exact
scenario/taxonomy/document snapshots are immutable. Changes create versions.
